# Maple Robotics Procurement Policies

## POL-01 Renewal Price Increases

Software and service renewals must not increase the annual price by more than 7% over the prior term for the same scope. Any renewal above 7% requires written justification from the vendor and approval from Finance before signature. Bundled add-ons must be priced separately so the like-for-like increase is visible.

## POL-02 Auto-Renewal Clauses

Any auto-renewal clause must be flagged to the requester and to Procurement before signature. Maple Robotics requires a non-renewal notice period of at least 90 days. Clauses that let the vendor set future renewal prices at its then-current list price are not acceptable.

## POL-03 Data Residency

Customer data from Canadian customers must be stored and processed in Canada. Customer data from EU customers must be stored and processed in the EU. Vendors that cannot meet these requirements may not receive customer data, and an exception requires approval from the Privacy Officer.

## POL-04 Limitation of Liability

Vendor contracts must cap each party's liability at no more than twelve months of fees, with standard carve-outs for confidentiality and data breaches. Maple Robotics must never accept unlimited liability or broad indemnities in favour of the vendor. Legal must review any liability clause that departs from this standard.

## POL-05 Governing Law and Disputes

Contracts must be governed by the laws of Ontario, with disputes resolved in Toronto, Ontario. Mandatory arbitration outside Ontario, or arbitration rules chosen by the vendor alone, must be escalated to Legal before signature.

## POL-06 Duplicate Vendors

Before a new vendor is created, Accounts Payable must check for existing vendors with a similar name, the same email domain, the same address, or the same tax number. A possible duplicate must be reviewed before setup. Any request to change bank details must be verified by phone using contact details already on file, never details supplied in the request.

## POL-07 Approval Thresholds

Purchases under CAD 10,000 need manager approval. Purchases from CAD 10,000 to under CAD 50,000 need director approval. Purchases of CAD 50,000 or more need approval from a VP and from Finance.

## POL-08 Security Review

Any vendor that will store or process customer data must pass a security review before onboarding. The vendor must provide a current SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certificate and complete the Maple Robotics security questionnaire.
