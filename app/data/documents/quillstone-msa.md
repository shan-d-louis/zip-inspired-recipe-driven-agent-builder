# Quillstone Consulting Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is between Quillstone Consulting Ltd. ("Consultant"), a British Columbia company, and Maple Robotics Inc. ("Client"). It applies to every statement of work ("SOW") signed under it.

## 1. Services

Consultant will provide the professional services described in each SOW. Consultant will assign qualified staff and may use subcontractors with Client's prior written consent.

## 2. Fees and Expenses

Client will pay the fees in each SOW. Reasonable travel expenses are billed at cost with receipts. Invoices are issued monthly and are payable within 30 days.

## 3. Client Responsibilities

Client will provide timely access to systems, data and staff needed for the services. Delays caused by Client may change the schedule and fees in the SOW.

## 4. Intellectual Property

Consultant keeps ownership of its pre-existing tools and methods. Deliverables created specifically for Client under an SOW become Client's property on full payment.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other's confidential information with at least reasonable care and use it only for this Agreement.

## 6. Liability

Consultant's liability under this Agreement shall be unlimited, and the Client shall indemnify and hold Consultant harmless against all third-party claims arising from Client's use of the deliverables. Neither party excludes liability for indirect or consequential damages.

## 7. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved by mandatory binding arbitration seated in Vancouver, British Columbia, under the rules of a recognized arbitration body chosen by Consultant. This Agreement is governed by the laws of British Columbia. Client waives the right to bring claims in court.

## 8. Term and Termination

This Agreement continues until terminated. Either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice. Client will pay for services performed up to the termination date.

## 9. Non-Solicitation

During the term and for 12 months after, Client will not hire Consultant staff who worked on the services.
