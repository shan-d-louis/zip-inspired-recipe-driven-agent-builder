# Fjord Cloud Storage Security Overview

Fjord Cloud Storage provides durable, low-cost archive storage for logs, telemetry and backups. This overview summarizes the controls that protect customer data.

## Certifications

Fjord maintains a SOC 2 Type II report covering security, availability and confidentiality, renewed every year by an independent auditor. Fjord also aligns its program with ISO 27001, although it is not certified.

## Encryption

All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Customers on the Enterprise plan can bring their own keys through a supported key management service. Encryption keys are rotated every 12 months.

## Access Control

Customer access uses single sign-on with SAML 2.0 and supports role-based access. Fjord employees cannot access customer data without a support ticket approved by the customer. All administrative access is logged and reviewed weekly.

## Availability

The archive tier is designed for 99.9% availability and 11 nines of durability. Data is replicated across two facilities. Restore requests for archived objects complete within 12 hours.

## Incident Response

Fjord runs a 24/7 security operations team. Confirmed incidents affecting customer data are reported within 72 hours, in line with the Data Processing Addendum.

## Vulnerability Management

An external firm performs penetration testing every year. Critical vulnerabilities are patched within 7 days and high vulnerabilities within 30 days.

## Employee Security

Employees complete security training on hire and every year after. Background checks are performed where permitted by law.

## Contact

Security questions can be sent to the Fjord security team through the customer portal. A summary of the latest SOC 2 report is available under NDA.
