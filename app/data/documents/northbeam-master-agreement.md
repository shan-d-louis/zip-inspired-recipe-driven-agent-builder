# Northbeam Analytics Master Subscription Agreement (C-2025-014)

This Master Subscription Agreement is entered into on November 1, 2025 between Maple Robotics Inc. ("Customer"), an Ontario corporation, and Northbeam Analytics ("Provider"). It governs Customer's use of the Northbeam analytics platform for factory telemetry dashboards.

## 1. Term

The initial term begins on November 1, 2025 and ends on October 31, 2026. This Agreement does not renew automatically. Any renewal requires a new order form signed by both parties before the end of the current term.

## 2. Fees

Customer will pay an annual subscription fee of CAD 55,000 for up to 40 named users and 2 production workspaces. Fees are invoiced annually in advance and payable within 30 days of invoice.

## 3. Price Protection

For any renewal of this Agreement, annual fee increases shall not exceed seven percent (7%) of the fees paid in the immediately preceding term. This cap applies to the same products, quantities and service levels. New products or additional users may be quoted separately at Provider's then-current list price, but must be itemized so that Customer can see the price of the existing scope.

## 4. Notice of Non-Renewal

Either party may decline to renew by giving written notice at least ninety (90) days before the end of the current term. Provider will send a renewal quote no later than 75 days before the end of the term.

## 5. Data and Security

Provider hosts Customer data in its Canadian region (Montreal). Provider maintains a SOC 2 Type II report and will provide it on request. Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## 6. Liability

Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months before the event giving rise to the claim, except for breaches of confidentiality.

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario. Disputes will be resolved in the courts of Toronto, Ontario.

Signed for Maple Robotics Inc. by the VP Finance, and for Northbeam Analytics by its Head of Sales.
