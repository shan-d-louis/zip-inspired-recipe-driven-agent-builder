# Fjord Cloud Storage Data Processing Addendum

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Fjord Cloud Storage Terms of Service between Fjord Cloud Storage, Inc. ("Processor") and the customer named in the order form ("Controller").

## 1. Scope

Processor will process Customer Data only to provide the archive storage service described in the order form and only on Controller's documented instructions. Customer Data includes files, logs and metadata uploaded by Controller.

## 2. Data Location and Residency

Customer Data will be stored and processed exclusively in data centers located in the United States. Processor does not offer data residency in Canada, the EU or any other region outside the United States for the archive storage tier. Backups are replicated between Processor's Oregon and Virginia facilities.

## 3. Subprocessors

Processor uses the subprocessors listed on its website, including its infrastructure provider and its support ticketing provider. Processor will give Controller 15 days' notice of any new subprocessor. Controller may object, and if the parties cannot resolve the objection, Controller may terminate the affected service.

## 4. International Transfers

Where Customer Data originates outside the United States, Controller is responsible for ensuring that it has a lawful basis to transfer that data to the United States. Processor will sign standard contractual clauses on request.

## 5. Security

Processor will maintain the technical and organizational measures described in its Security Overview, including encryption at rest and in transit, access logging, and annual penetration testing.

## 6. Breach Notification

Processor will notify Controller of a confirmed personal data breach without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

## 7. Deletion

On termination, Processor will delete Customer Data within 90 days unless law requires longer retention. Controller may export its data at any time before deletion.

## 8. Audit

Processor will make its SOC 2 Type II report available once per year. On-site audits are not offered.
