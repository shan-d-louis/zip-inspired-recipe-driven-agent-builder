# Statement of Work #1: ERP Migration Planning and Rollout

This SOW is issued under the Master Services Agreement between Quillstone Consulting Ltd. and Maple Robotics Inc.

## Objective

Plan and lead the migration of Maple Robotics' finance and procurement processes from its legacy accounting system to a new cloud ERP, with go-live before the start of the next fiscal year.

## Scope

Consultant will run discovery workshops with Finance, Procurement and Operations; document current and future processes; design the chart of accounts and approval workflows; configure the ERP modules for general ledger, accounts payable and purchasing; lead data migration rehearsals; and support user acceptance testing and go-live.

## Out of Scope

Payroll, manufacturing execution systems, custom software development, and hardware procurement are out of scope. Changes to scope require a signed change order.

## Team

One engagement lead, one senior functional consultant, and one data migration specialist. Consultant staff will work mostly remotely, with up to four on-site visits to the Waterloo plant.
